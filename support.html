Frequently asked questions

I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads, or I reinstalled the game.

Open the game’s Settings and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Google account you bought with. Coins and other consumable items are not restored this way, because they were already added to your game.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress is saved on your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, your progress is restored when you set up the new phone with the same account and install the game from Google Play.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for Google Play purchases are handled by Google. You can request one from Google Play’s refund page.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings to delete everything stored on your phone. For anything else, see our Privacy Policy or email us.
