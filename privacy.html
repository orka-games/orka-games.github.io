CONTACT US

If you have questions about data protection, or if you have any requests for resolving issues with your personal data, you can contact us at games.orka@gmail.com. Orka Games is run by an individual developer, Orhan Kırcı, who is the controller of your personal data.

THE DATA WE COLLECT

The categories of personal data we collect depend on how you use the Service and the requirements of applicable law. The Service does not require you to create an account.

Data you provide us directly.

  • Your email address and the content of your messages when you contact us for support. If you use Support in the game's Settings, the message also includes the app version, your device model and Android version, the game language and your anonymous purchase ID.
  • Our payment service provider, Google Play, collects your payment details in connection with any purchases you make in the Service. We never receive your payment card details.

Data we collect automatically.

  • Data about your game progress, coins, boosters, owned items and settings. This data is stored on your device.
  • Gameplay statistics, such as levels started, completed, failed or restarted, time spent on a level, boosters used or obtained, rewarded videos watched, Lucky Wheel spins and tutorial steps seen. These are linked to a random app instance ID created by our analytics provider, not to your name, email or advertising ID.
  • An automatically created anonymous ID used to record and restore your purchases
  • Your IP address and mobile device identifiers (such as your advertising ID and app set ID)
  • Data about your device, such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • Approximate location data (as derived from IP address)
  • Data about your use of the Service, such as purchases made, ads viewed and interacted with, and diagnostic data (such as app launch and performance information)

Data we collect from our partners.

  • Data from the platform the games run on and from payment service providers (such as purchase verification data from Google Play)
  • Data to fight fraud (such as refund abuse in games or click fraud in advertising)

Data we do not collect.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location. The Service has no chat, player profiles or social login.

WHY DO WE COLLECT YOUR DATA

We use your data for the following purposes:

To make the Service work.

To perform the contract, we process data necessary to:

  • Allow you to play our games and use the Service
  • Save your game progress on your device
  • Verify and confirm payments
  • Provide and deliver products you purchase, and restore them on a new install (for example, Remove Ads)

To support you and improve the Service.

We have a legitimate interest to process necessary data to:

  • Respond to your comments and questions and provide player support
  • Find and fix problems and improve the Service and player experience
  • Measure how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard) using gameplay statistics. Where the law requires consent for this (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we collect gameplay statistics only with your consent.

To show advertisements.

The Service shows ads, including optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To show, measure and improve ads, we and our advertising partner process necessary data. Personalized advertisements are shown only with your consent where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland); otherwise we rely on our legitimate interest. For information on how to opt out of personalized advertisements, see “Your Rights and Options” below.

To keep the Service safe and fair.

To fight fraud, we have a legitimate interest to process necessary data to detect invalid purchases, refund abuse and invalid ad traffic, and take action against them.

With your consent.

With your consent, we may process your data for additional purposes.

YOUR RIGHTS AND OPTIONS

We do not send marketing emails or push notifications.

Opt out of targeted advertising.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising by changing your ad privacy choices in the game’s settings where that option is shown (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), and by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your device’s ad settings (for example Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Access the personal data we hold about you.

If you request, we will provide you a copy of the personal data we hold about you in an electronic format.

Your other rights.

You also have the right to correct your data, have your data deleted, object to how we use or share your data, and restrict how we use or share your data. You can always withdraw your consent. You can delete all game data stored on your device at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your Android settings.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, contact us as described in “Contact Us” above. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law. You may also designate an authorized agent to exercise your rights on your behalf, as permitted by applicable law.

If you have an unresolved privacy concern, you may contact your local data protection authority, for example in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, or in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

We do not use cookies in our games or on this website. Our advertising partner uses mobile identifiers (such as the advertising ID and app set ID) and similar technologies to show ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. Our analytics provider stores a random app instance ID on your device to count gameplay statistics; it does not use your advertising ID. These partners operate under their own privacy policies.

HOW DO WE PROTECT YOUR DATA

Security safeguards.

Data sent by the Service to our service providers is encrypted in transit. Game data stored on your device is protected by your device’s security. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

Data retention.

Game data on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Gameplay statistics are kept by our analytics provider for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Our other service providers retain data according to their own retention policies. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request. If you ask us to remove your personal data, we may still retain data as necessary to comply with our legal obligations (such as keeping purchase records), resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

CHILDREN

Protecting children’s privacy online is very important to us. Our Service is not directed to children under 13 (or other age as required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, you may contact us as described above. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it unless we have a legal obligation to keep it.
