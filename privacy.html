Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snake Yarn Jam and other games published by Orka Games (together, the “Games”) handle your information. We keep this short and specific: the Games do not require an account, and most of what they store never leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Orka Games is operated by Orhan Kırcı, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the processing described here.

Contact: games.orka@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, boosters, purchases you own, and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved on your device so the game works and you can continue where you left off. We do not receive this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is stored and managed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Information we do not collect

The Games have no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. Google Play’s handling of your data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and to restore the items you own (for example, Remove Ads) on a new install. For this, RevenueCat processes a random app user ID generated on your device, your purchase records (product, price, currency, date, store transaction ID), basic device and app information (operating system, app version, store country) and your IP address. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising

The Games show ads through Google AdMob, for example optional ads you can watch for a reward. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (used to estimate a coarse location) and information about the ads you see and interact with, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Games ask for it with Google’s consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice from the game’s settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy Remove Ads, the Games stop showing ads that you did not choose to watch.

6. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

8. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google and RevenueCat), who may process it in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

9. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the game or clear its data. Google and RevenueCat keep data according to their own retention policies. Purchase records may be kept as long as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to ask what data is processed about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email games.orka@gmail.com. Because the Games have no accounts, please include your purchase order number (GPA.xxxx) if your request concerns purchases, so we can find the related records. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

You can delete all game data on your device at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings.

11. Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the Games change. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will also be noted in the game where appropriate.
